## 正文

We tend to think of smartphones, laptops and smartwatches as useful tools that make our lives easier. But these devices were not created by nature — they are artefacts designed by human beings with specific purposes in mind. And one of those purposes, according to an increasing number of critics, is surveillance.

Every piece of technology is designed to do something, and that fact alone strips it of neutrality. Pillows are supposed to be comfortable; scissors are meant to cut; and digital devices, as they are currently designed, are built to collect data about their users. This is not an accident — it is a design choice.

Technology companies have been promoting a popular narrative: technology itself is neutral, and it is our use of it that determines whether it is good or bad. This argument sounds reasonable at first, but it is deeply misleading. A blank page is not even neutral — it invites you to write. Technology is never truly neutral because it always embodies the values of its designers.

Consider what your phone contains: a microphone, a camera, an accelerometer, a compass, a barometer, a light sensor and a GPS, among many other sensors. Many apps on our devices — maps, social media and online documents — were designed specifically to generate personal data. The fact that these apps are useful to us is not a coincidence; it is the attraction that makes us voluntarily give up information that is then used to track our behaviour.

The purpose of all this data collection is prediction. Companies gather enormous amounts of personal data so they can predict what we will buy, where we will go, and what we will do next. They then sell these predictions to advertisers, or use them to influence our choices directly.

But there is a deeper problem. When people know they are being watched, they tend to change their behaviour. In authoritarian countries, surveillance is used to keep citizens in line. In democratic societies, the effect is more subtle but no less real: people become more cautious about what they say, what they search for and whom they associate with. Over time, this chips away at the freedom that democracy depends on.

Moreover, predictions about people have a troubling tendency to become self-fulfilling. If an algorithm predicts that a student will fail, teachers may pay less attention to that student, making the failure more likely. If a system predicts someone will commit a crime, that person may be denied opportunities that could have kept them on a different path.

Healthy democracies require uncertainty. Elections are meaningful only because we do not already know the results. If we allow surveillance and prediction to eliminate that uncertainty, we risk losing something far more valuable than convenience.

## 长难句解析

### 句子1

> The fact that these apps are useful to us is not a coincidence; it is the attraction that makes us voluntarily give up information that is then used to track our behaviour.

**拆分**：
- **分句1**: The fact that these apps are useful to us is not a coincidence（这些应用对我们有用这一事实并非巧合）
  - that these apps are useful to us 是同位语从句，解释 the fact
- **分句2**: it is the attraction that makes us voluntarily give up information（它是一种诱饵，使我们自愿交出信息）
  - that is then used to track our behaviour 是定语从句，修饰 information

**翻译**: 这些应用对我们有用并非巧合；它是一种诱饵，使我们自愿交出随后被用于追踪我们行为的信息。

### 句子2

> If a system predicts someone will commit a crime, that person may be denied opportunities that could have kept them on a different path.

**拆分**：
- **条件状语从句**: If a system predicts someone will commit a crime
- **主句**: that person may be denied opportunities
- **定语从句**: that could have kept them on a different path（本来可以让他们走上不同道路的[机会]）
  - could have done 表示虚拟语气，"本可以"

**翻译**: 如果一个系统预测某人会犯罪，这个人可能会被剥夺那些本可以让他走上不同道路的机会。

## 文章大意

本文论证数字设备并非中立工具，而是被设计来收集用户数据以进行预测。作者指出"技术中立论"是科技公司误导公众的说法，数据收集的真正目的是预测和影响行为。文章警告，监控和预测会侵蚀民主社会所依赖的自由和不确定性。

## 思考题

1. According to the passage, why is the argument that "technology is neutral" misleading?
   - A) Because technology is too complex for ordinary people to understand
   - B) Because technology always embodies the values and purposes of its designers
   - C) Because users always misuse technology for bad purposes
   - D) Because technology companies are not honest about their products

2. The phrase "chip away at" in paragraph 6 is closest in meaning to:
   - A) suddenly destroy
   - B) gradually weaken
   - C) completely protect
   - D) carefully examine

3. What does the author suggest about predictions in paragraph 7?
   - A) Predictions are always accurate and helpful
   - B) Predictions can influence behaviour in ways that make the predictions come true
   - C) Predictions should replace human judgement in all areas
   - D) Predictions are only problematic in authoritarian countries

<details>
<summary>参考答案</summary>

1. **B** — "Technology is never truly neutral because it always embodies the values of its designers"，技术不是中立的，因为它体现了设计者的价值观。

2. **B** — chip away at 意为"逐渐侵蚀、一点一点地削弱"，与 gradually weaken 同义。

3. **B** — 文中指出 "predictions about people have a troubling tendency to become self-fulfilling"，预测会变成自证预言，即预测本身影响行为使其成真。
