# 2017 Text 4 — 野火与气候变化

## 正文

Wildfires have always been a part of nature's cycle. Many ecosystems depend on periodic burning to clear dead vegetation, recycle nutrients, and trigger new growth. But something has changed. Over the past two decades, wildfires have become more frequent, more intense, and more destructive than at any time in recorded history. The question is no longer whether climate change is influencing fire behaviour — it is how fast the damage will escalate.

The data is unambiguous. In the United States, the average annual area burned by wildfires has tripled since the 1990s. Australia's 2019–2020 bushfire season destroyed an area larger than England, killing an estimated three billion animals. In 2023, Canada experienced its worst fire season ever, with flames consuming 18 million hectares and sending smoke across the eastern seaboard of the United States. Southern Europe, Siberia, and the Amazon have all witnessed unprecedented fire activity. These are not isolated events; they form a consistent global pattern.

Climate change drives this trend through multiple mechanisms. Rising temperatures dry out vegetation faster, creating more fuel for fires. Extended droughts — once rare — have become routine in many regions. In the American West, the fire season is now two to three months longer than it was a generation ago. Hotter nights, which once gave firefighters a window to contain blazes, now offer little relief. Wind patterns are shifting in ways that spread fires more rapidly across larger areas.

Yet climate is only part of the story. Decades of aggressive fire suppression have left many forests choked with dense undergrowth that would have been cleared naturally by smaller, low-intensity fires. Human settlement in fire-prone areas — the so-called wildland-urban interface — has expanded dramatically, putting more lives and property at risk while also making it harder to allow natural fires to burn. In the United States, roughly one-third of all homes are now located in these zones.

The debate over how to respond is contentious. Some experts advocate for prescribed burns — deliberately setting controlled fires during safe conditions to reduce fuel loads. Indigenous communities practised this for thousands of years, and studies confirm that landscapes managed with fire are more resilient. Critics, however, warn that prescribed burns carry their own risks: they can escape control, damage timber reserves, and release smoke that harms air quality. Insurance companies and residents near forests often object, fearing liability and property damage.

What is not in dispute is that the current approach is failing. Spending on fire suppression now consumes more than half of the US Forest Service's budget, crowding out prevention and restoration programmes. A new strategy is needed — one that accepts fire as an inevitable feature of a warming world, invests in making communities fire-resistant, and addresses the root cause by reducing greenhouse gas emissions. The flames will keep coming. The only question is whether we will be prepared.

## 长难句解析

**1.** Decades of aggressive fire suppression have left many forests choked with dense undergrowth that would have been cleared naturally by smaller, low-intensity fires.

- **拆分**：主句为 decades of fire suppression have left forests choked with...，过去分词短语 choked with... 作宾语补足语；that 引导定语从句修饰 undergrowth，其中使用了虚拟语气 would have been cleared。
- **翻译**：数十年来积极的灭火行动使许多森林被密集的灌木堵塞，而这些灌木本可以被较小规模的低强度自然火灾清除。

**2.** A new strategy is needed — one that accepts fire as an inevitable feature of a warming world, invests in making communities fire-resistant, and addresses the root cause by reducing greenhouse gas emissions.

- **拆分**：one 指代 strategy，后接三个并列谓语 accepts, invests, addresses；that 引导定语从句修饰 one。
- **翻译**：我们需要一种新策略——承认火灾是变暖世界中不可避免的特征，投资使社区具备防火能力，并通过减少温室气体排放来应对根本原因。

## 文章大意

本文论述了全球野火日益频繁和严重的现象及其与气候变化的关系。自1990年代以来，美国年均过火面积增至三倍，澳大利亚和加拿大也经历了史无前例的火灾。气候变化通过升温干燥植被、延长干旱和火季、改变风向等机制加剧火灾。但气候只是部分原因——长期灭火政策导致森林灌木过密，人类向易火区扩张也增加了风险。应对措施存在争议：计划烧除虽有效但有风险。当前的灭火策略正在失败，需要承认火灾的不可避免性，增强社区防火能力，并从根本上减少排放。

## 思考题

**1.** According to the passage, which of the following contributes to the worsening wildfire situation?

A. Decreased global temperatures
B. Reduced human settlement in forest areas
C. Decades of aggressive fire suppression
D. Increased use of prescribed burns

**2.** The phrase "wildland-urban interface" (Paragraph 4) refers to:

A. the boundary between two national parks
B. areas where human settlements meet fire-prone natural landscapes
C. the technology used to detect wildfires near cities
D. the ecological zone between forests and grasslands

**3.** The author's main argument is that:

A. climate change is the sole cause of worsening wildfires
B. prescribed burns should be immediately adopted everywhere
C. fire suppression budgets should be increased
D. a comprehensive new strategy is needed that addresses both fire management and climate change

2. **B** — "野地-城市交界区"指人类居住区与易发火灾的自然景观相交汇的区域。
3. **D** — 作者主张需要一种综合性的新策略，既承认火灾的不可避免性、增强社区防火能力，又从根本上减少温室气体排放。
