## 正文

For decades, the encryption that protects everything from bank transfers to state secrets has relied on a subtle fiction: that computers can generate truly random numbers. In reality, classical computers are deterministic machines — they execute instructions step by step, and every output is logically determined by its inputs. The "random" numbers they produce are actually pseudorandom, generated by mathematical algorithms that, given enough information, are entirely predictable. Researchers at ETH Zurich have now demonstrated a system that generates genuine, verifiable randomness using quantum mechanics, and it could fundamentally strengthen digital security.

The ETH team's device uses just two qubits — the basic units of quantum information — separated by a distance of 30 metres. When a qubit is measured, its state collapses into one of two possible outcomes, and according to the principles of quantum mechanics, this collapse is inherently unpredictable. Unlike a classical algorithm that might produce the same sequence twice given the same seed, quantum measurement yields outcomes that no amount of prior knowledge can predict. The challenge is proving that the outcomes are genuinely random rather than merely appearing so — a distinction that matters enormously in cryptography, where a predictable key is a compromised key.

To verify the integrity of their random numbers, the researchers performed approximately 1.5 billion Bell tests. A Bell test checks whether the correlations between two entangled particles exceed what would be possible under any classical physics model. If they do — and the ETH results overwhelmingly confirmed this — then the measurement outcomes cannot have been predetermined by any hidden variables. In other words, the randomness is not just apparently random; it is physically, fundamentally random.

The practical implications are significant. Modern encryption depends on random number generation for creating cryptographic keys, session tokens, and authentication challenges. If an adversary can predict the output of a random number generator — as has happened in several documented security breaches — the entire encryption system collapses. A quantum random number generator that is provably unpredictable would eliminate this class of vulnerability entirely.

However, deploying the technology at scale remains challenging. The current system requires carefully controlled laboratory conditions, including ultra-low temperatures to maintain qubit coherence and sophisticated shielding against environmental interference. Each device is expensive and delicate, a far cry from the cheap microchips that power today's pseudorandom generators. The ETH team is working on miniaturised versions that could operate at higher temperatures, but widespread commercial availability is likely years away.

The deeper significance may be philosophical as much as practical. For centuries, scientists debated whether true randomness exists in nature or whether apparent randomness merely reflects human ignorance. The ETH experiment does not settle the metaphysical question, but it provides the strongest evidence yet that at the quantum level, the universe genuinely is indeterminate — and that this indeterminacy can be harnessed to protect our most sensitive information.

## 长难句解析

**1.** Unlike a classical algorithm that might produce the same sequence twice given the same seed, quantum measurement yields outcomes that no amount of prior knowledge can predict.

- **状语**: Unlike a classical algorithm that might produce the same sequence twice given the same seed
- **定语从句 1**: that might produce the same sequence twice given the same seed（修饰 algorithm）
- **主句**: quantum measurement yields outcomes
- **定语从句 2**: that no amount of prior knowledge can predict（修饰 outcomes）
- **翻译**: 与给定相同种子可能产生相同序列的经典算法不同，量子测量产生的结果，任何数量的先验知识都无法预测。

**2.** If they do — and the ETH results overwhelmingly confirmed this — then the measurement outcomes cannot have been predetermined by any hidden variables.

- **条件句**: If they do（如果它们确实如此）
- **插入语**: and the ETH results overwhelmingly confirmed this
- **主句**: then the measurement outcomes cannot have been predetermined by any hidden variables
- **被动语态 + 否定推断**: cannot have been predetermined（不可能已被预先确定）
- **翻译**: 如果确实如此——ETH的结果压倒性地证实了这一点——那么测量结果就不可能被任何隐变量预先确定。

## 文章大意（中文概括）

ETH Zurich团队用量子力学实现了真正可验证的随机数生成。经典计算机产生的"随机"数实为伪随机，可被预测。该团队用两个相隔30米的量子比特，通过量子测量坍缩产生真正不可预测的结果，并以15亿次贝尔测试验证其随机性——证明结果不受任何隐变量预先决定。这对加密意义重大，可消除随机数可预测性导致的安全漏洞。但当前设备需要极低温和精密屏蔽，距大规模商用尚需数年。更深层的意义是哲学性的：实验提供了量子层面宇宙真正不确定的有力证据。

## 思考题

**1.** Why are classical computer-generated random numbers considered "pseudorandom"?

A. Because they are generated too slowly for practical use.

B. Because they are produced by deterministic algorithms and are theoretically predictable.

C. Because they contain systematic biases that make them useless.

D. Because they are always the same regardless of the input.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。文章指出"classical computers are deterministic machines...every output is logically determined by its inputs"，因此产生的随机数是"pseudorandom...given enough information, are entirely predictable"。A原文未提及速度；C说系统性偏差使随机数无用，过于绝对；D说总是相同，忽略种子不同时结果不同。
</details>

**2.** What is the purpose of the 1.5 billion Bell tests performed by the ETH team?

A. To measure the speed of qubit operations.

B. To verify that the random outcomes are genuinely unpredictable and not predetermined.

C. To determine the optimal distance between qubits.

D. To test whether the equipment can withstand high temperatures.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文指出Bell test检查"whether the correlations between two entangled particles exceed what would be possible under any classical physics model"，如果超过则"the measurement outcomes cannot have been predetermined by any hidden variables"，即验证随机性是真正的、而非预先决定的。A、C、D与Bell test目的无关。
</details>

**3.** The word "harnessed" in the last paragraph is closest in meaning to ______.

A. ignored

B. controlled and put to practical use

C. theoretically debated

D. replaced by classical methods

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。"harness"意为"利用、驾驭"，原文说"this indeterminacy can be harnessed to protect our most sensitive information"，即量子不确定性可以被驾驭来保护敏感信息。A是反义；C原文已说明不限于理论争论；D与原文量子替代经典的方向相反。
</details>
