## 正文

When the pandemic forced offices to close in 2020, millions of employees began working from home for the first time. What started as an emergency measure has become a permanent shift in how we work. Yet more than three years later, a fundamental question remains unresolved: does remote work make people more or less productive? The answer, it turns out, depends on what you measure, how you measure it, and whom you ask.

Early research painted an optimistic picture. A widely cited study of a large Asian technology company found that remote workers were 13 percent more productive than their office-based counterparts, largely because they took fewer breaks and sick days, and because the quieter home environment allowed for longer periods of focused work. Another study, conducted by researchers at Stanford, found that call centre employees working from home completed 13 percent more calls per shift. These findings seemed to confirm what many remote workers had long suspected: offices, with their constant interruptions and pointless meetings, were the real obstacle to productivity.

But later studies have produced more nuanced results. A 2022 analysis by researchers at the University of Chicago found that while remote workers reported higher satisfaction and better work-life balance, their output on certain collaborative tasks was lower than that of in-person teams. The mechanisms are instructive. Remote work makes spontaneous interactions — the hallway conversation that sparks an idea, the quick question that prevents a mistake — far less likely. Communication becomes deliberate and scheduled, which improves efficiency for routine tasks but inhibits the kind of informal problem-solving that drives innovation. Studies of Microsoft employees found that remote work made teams more siloed, with weaker cross-departmental connections and less sharing of new information.

The hybrid model — typically two to three days in the office per week — has emerged as a compromise that captures many of the benefits of both arrangements. Research from the MIT Sloan School of Management suggests that hybrid workers report productivity levels comparable to fully in-office workers while enjoying the flexibility and reduced commuting time of remote work. However, hybrid work is not without challenges. Coordinating schedules so that team members overlap in the office on the same days requires careful planning. And there is evidence that remote days tend to be overrepresented on Mondays and Fridays, raising concerns about "coast days" rather than genuine productivity.

The debate over remote work is ultimately a debate about what we value. If productivity means completing defined tasks efficiently, remote work often wins. If it means fostering creativity, building relationships, and developing talent, the office retains important advantages. Most organisations will need both. The companies that thrive will be those that design work arrangements with intentionality — choosing when to gather in person and when to work remotely based on the nature of the task, not habit or dogma.

## 长难句解析

**1.** Remote work makes spontaneous interactions — the hallway conversation that sparks an idea, the quick question that prevents a mistake — far less likely.

- **拆分**：主句为 remote work makes spontaneous interactions far less likely，破折号之间为两个并列名词短语举例说明 spontaneous interactions，各含 that 引导的定语从句。
- **翻译**：远程工作使自发互动——激发灵感的走廊交谈、防止错误的快速提问——变得可能性大大降低。

**2.** The companies that thrive will be those that design work arrangements with intentionality — choosing when to gather in person and when to work remotely based on the nature of the task, not habit or dogma.

- **拆分**：两个 that 分别引导定语从句修饰 companies 和 those；choosing... 为现在分词作伴随状语，when to... 为不定式短语作宾语；based on... 为条件状语。
- **翻译**：蓬勃发展的公司将是那些有目的地设计工作安排的公司——根据任务性质而非习惯或教条来选择何时面对面、何时远程工作。

## 文章大意

本文探讨了远程工作与生产力的关系。早期研究显示远程工作效率更高——减少休息和病假、安静环境有利专注，效率提升13%。但后续研究发现远程工作在协作任务上表现较差：自发互动减少，沟通变得刻意和程式化，团队更加割裂，跨部门联系和信息分享减弱。混合办公模式（每周两三天到办公室）成为折中方案，但也有协调困难的问题。文章认为，关于远程工作的争论本质上是关于我们重视什么：若生产力意味着高效完成既定任务，远程工作更优；若意味着培养创造力和人才，办公室仍有优势。
