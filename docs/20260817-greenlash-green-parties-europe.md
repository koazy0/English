## 正文

European Green parties have stalled. After a historic breakthrough in the 2019 European elections, when Green candidates surged across the continent on a wave of climate activism, the movement has lost momentum. Vote shares have flatlined or declined in subsequent national elections in Germany, France, and the Netherlands. The explanation, analysts suggest, lies in a phenomenon increasingly described as "greenlash" — a popular backlash against climate policies perceived as costly, inconvenient, or imposed by urban elites upon rural and working-class communities.

The dynamics of greenlash are complex but traceable. Carbon taxes on fuel, bans on gas boilers, restrictions on short-haul flights, and mandates for electric vehicles have all generated fierce opposition from voters who feel the costs of the green transition are being distributed unfairly. Farmers' protests across Europe, which saw tractors blockading city streets from Brussels to Berlin, crystallised the grievance: that environmental regulation threatens livelihoods without offering adequate compensation or alternatives.

Yet the story is not uniformly one of decline. The Green Party of England and Wales, under the co-leadership of Zack Polanski, has defied the continental trend, surging in local elections and membership numbers. The party's approach offers three instructive lessons.

First, emphasise the economy. While continental Greens led with climate policy, Polanski's Greens foreground economic inequality, housing shortages, and the cost of living. Climate action was framed not as a sacrifice but as an opportunity — green jobs, lower energy bills through home insulation, and reduced dependence on volatile fossil fuel markets. The message resonated with voters who support environmental goals in principle but prioritise immediate economic concerns in practice.

Second, be ambitious, not moderate. Counterintuitively, the Green Party gained ground not by softening its positions but by sharpening them. A wealth tax, rent controls, and public ownership of utilities were hardly moderate proposals, yet they attracted voters disillusioned with the bland centrism of established parties. Ambitious policies, the results suggest, signal conviction and differentiate a party from competitors who offer only incremental tinkering.

Third, take clear progressive stances on social issues. While some European Greens attempted to broaden appeal by soft-pedalling on immigration and social justice, the UK Greens leaned into progressive positions — defending migrants' rights, advocating for trans rights, and opposing austerity. This approach risked alienating conservative-leaning voters but solidified support among younger and urban demographics who might otherwise have drifted to progressive parties further left.

The broader implication is clear. Green parties do not fail because climate concern has diminished — polls consistently show rising public worry about environmental degradation. They fail when they allow climate policy to be framed as a burden rather than a benefit, and when they neglect the economic anxieties that dominate most voters' daily lives. The path back to relevance runs through the kitchen table, not just the climate summit.

## 文章大意（中文概括）

欧洲绿党在2019年历史性突破后陷入停滞，"绿色反弹"现象解释了这一衰退：碳税、燃气锅炉禁令等政策被认为代价不公，农民抗议集中体现了不满。但英国绿党在Zack Polanski领导下逆势上扬，提供三点启示：一、主打经济——将气候行动定义为机会而非牺牲；二、走激进路线而非温和路线——大胆政策彰显信念，区别于温和派的修修补补；三、坚持进步社会立场——捍卫移民权利和反紧缩。核心启示是：绿党失败不是因为公众不关心气候，而是因为让气候政策被塑造为负担，忽视了选民的经济焦虑。

## 思考题

**1.** What does "greenlash" refer to in the passage?

A. Environmental activists' anger at government inaction on climate change.

B. A popular backlash against climate policies seen as unfair or burdensome.

C. Green parties' internal conflicts over policy direction.

D. The rapid growth of renewable energy displacing fossil fuel jobs.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文定义greenlash为"a popular backlash against climate policies perceived as costly, inconvenient, or imposed by urban elites upon rural and working-class communities"，即公众对被认为不公平或有负担的气候政策的反弹。A是环保人士的愤怒，非公众反弹；C是党内矛盾，非公众反应；D是能源转型影响，非政策反弹。
</details>

**2.** According to the passage, why did the UK Green Party succeed where continental Greens stalled?

A. Because it abandoned climate policy entirely to focus on economic issues.

B. Because it softened its positions to attract moderate voters.

C. Because it framed climate action as an economic opportunity and took ambitious, progressive stances.

D. Because it received endorsement from major business leaders.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>C</b>。文章总结三点启示：强调经济（climate action framed as opportunity not sacrifice）、走激进路线（ambitious not moderate）、坚持进步立场（clear progressive stances）。A说"放弃气候政策"，与"foreground"矛盾；B说"软化立场"，与"sharpening positions"矛盾；D原文未提及商业支持。
</details>

**3.** The phrase "the path back to relevance runs through the kitchen table" suggests that ______.

A. Green parties should focus on domestic cooking and food policy

B. Green parties need to address everyday economic concerns rather than only climate summits

C. voters care more about environmental issues than economic ones

D. political meetings should be held in people's homes

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。"kitchen table"是隐喻，指家庭日常经济关切（如生活成本、住房），与"climate summit"（高层气候峰会）形成对比。作者认为绿党要重获关注必须从选民的日常经济关切出发。A字面理解错误；C与文章主旨相反；D曲解了比喻。
</details>
