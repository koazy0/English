# Pope Leo XIV's AI Encyclical: Disarming the Infrastructure of Power

> **Date**: 20260715 · **Source**: Adapted from Wired (May 2026) · **Difficulty**: ★★★★☆ · **Type**: 📰 同源外刊精选
>
> **Topic**: #科技 #伦理 #宗教

When Pope Leo XIV released the encyclical "Magnifica Humanitas" in May 2026, it marked the first time a pontiff had dedicated an entire papal letter to artificial intelligence. The document, running to nearly 40,000 words, does not merely urge caution or call for ethical guidelines. It frames AI as an infrastructure of power—a technological system that, left unchecked, will concentrate authority in the hands of a few global actors while diminishing the agency of billions.

The encyclical's central concept is what the Pope calls "disarming technology." The term is deliberately chosen. Just as the Church has historically advocated for the reduction of nuclear arsenals, Leo XIV argues for reducing the capacity of AI systems to dominate human existence. Disarming does not mean slowing technological progress or halting innovation; it means ensuring that AI serves human flourishing rather than subordinating human beings to algorithmic logic. "A technology that commands rather than serves has become an idol," the Pope writes, invoking theological language that carries particular weight within the Catholic tradition.

The document is especially pointed in its analysis of power concentration. The Pope observes that the development of frontier AI systems requires resources—vast computational infrastructure, enormous datasets, and exceptional engineering talent—that only a handful of corporations and governments possess. This concentration, he warns, creates a form of technological oligarchy in which decisions affecting billions are made by entities accountable to shareholders rather than citizens. The analogy to medieval feudalism is not made explicitly, but it hovers unmistakably beneath the surface.

Leo XIV also addresses the question of AI's impact on labour. While acknowledging that automation has historically created new forms of employment, he argues that the current wave of AI-driven displacement is different in both speed and scope. Workers whose skills are rendered obsolete by AI face not merely temporary inconvenience but existential uncertainty. The encyclical calls for a "just transition" that includes retraining, social protection, and—more controversially—the redistribution of wealth generated by AI productivity gains.

The response from the technology industry has been mixed. Some leaders welcomed the encyclical as a constructive contribution to the global conversation. Others bristled at the implicit criticism, arguing that the Church does not possess the technical expertise to evaluate AI systems. This objection somewhat misses the point. The Pope is not offering engineering recommendations; he is articulating a moral framework for evaluating whether a technology serves the common good. The question is not whether a neural network is well-designed but whether the system it enables concentrates or distributes power.

Perhaps the most striking aspect of "Magnifica Humanitas" is its insistence that technology is never neutral. Every design decision encodes values—about who matters, whose data is collected, whose interests are prioritised. The encyclical challenges the convenient fiction that AI is merely a tool whose consequences depend entirely on how it is used. Tools, the Pope reminds us, are shaped by the hands that build them, and those hands are never empty of intention.

## 长难句解析

> **The development of frontier AI systems requires resources**—vast computational infrastructure, enormous datasets, and exceptional engineering talent—**that only a handful of corporations and governments possess**, **this concentration, he warns, creates a form of technological oligarchy** in which decisions affecting billions are made by entities accountable to shareholders rather than citizens.

- **主干**: The development requires resources; this concentration creates a form of technological oligarchy
- **that only a handful of corporations and governments possess** 是定语从句，修饰 resources。
- **in which decisions affecting billions are made by entities accountable to shareholders rather than citizens** 是定语从句，修饰 oligarchy，说明"影响数十亿人的决策由对股东而非公民负责的实体做出"。
- **affecting billions** 是现在分词短语做后置定语，修饰 decisions。
- **翻译**: 前沿AI系统的开发需要资源——庞大的计算基础设施、海量数据集和卓越的工程人才——而这些资源只有少数企业和政府拥有；他警告说，这种集中造成了一种技术寡头制，影响数十亿人的决策由对股东而非公民负责的实体做出。

> **The encyclical challenges the convenient fiction that AI is merely a tool whose consequences depend entirely on how it is used**.

- **主干**: The encyclical challenges the convenient fiction
- **that AI is merely a tool** 是同位语从句，解释 fiction 的内容。
- **whose consequences depend entirely on how it is used** 是定语从句，修饰 tool。
- **how it is used** 是宾语从句，作 depend on 的宾语。
- **convenient fiction** 意为"方便的虚构"，即一种虽然不真实但被人乐于接受的假象。
- **翻译**: 这道通谕挑战了一种令人方便的虚构——即AI仅仅是一个工具，其后果完全取决于如何使用它。

## 文章大意

本文分析教皇利奥十四世的 AI 通谕《Magnifica Humanitas》。通谕首次将 AI 定位为"权力基础设施"，核心概念是"解除技术武装"——不是阻碍创新，而是防止 AI 支配人类存在。通谕特别批评了 AI 发展导致的权力集中，指出前沿 AI 所需的巨大资源只有少数企业和政府拥有，形成技术寡头。在劳动方面，通谕呼吁"公正转型"和财富再分配。行业反应不一，但作者指出教皇不是提供工程建议，而是道德框架。通谕最引人注目的观点是：技术从来不是中立的，每个设计决策都编码了价值观。
