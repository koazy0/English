# Climate Impacts Spiralling as Record Heat Becomes the Norm

## 正文

The World Meteorological Organization has issued its starkest assessment yet: there is an 86 per cent chance that a new record for the hottest year in human history will be set before the end of this decade. The probability that the five-year average temperature will exceed the critical 1.5 degrees Celsius threshold above pre-industrial levels has risen to 75 per cent, a figure that would have been considered alarmist merely a decade ago.

The projections are grounded in data that is becoming impossible to ignore. Global average temperatures have risen approximately 1.3 degrees above pre-industrial levels, and the rate of warming has accelerated markedly since 2020. Ocean temperatures, in particular, have shattered records repeatedly, driving more intense cyclones, disrupting marine ecosystems, and accelerating ice sheet loss in both Greenland and Antarctica. The WMO's analysis incorporates updated climate models that account for feedback loops previously underestimated, including the release of methane from thawing permafrost and the diminishing reflectivity of Arctic ice.

An El Niño event expected in late 2026 adds further urgency. Historically, El Niño years tend to be the hottest on record, as warm water in the Pacific releases additional heat into the atmosphere. Scientists warn that the combination of long-term warming trends and a strong El Niño could push 2027 to unprecedented temperatures, with cascading consequences for agriculture, water supplies, and public health across the tropics and subtropics.

The UN's climate chief did not mince words in response. Describing the current spate of global heatwaves as a "brutal reminder," she called on governments to honour their commitments under the Paris Agreement and accelerate the phase-out of fossil fuels. "We are not witnessing an aberration," she said. "We are witnessing the predictable outcome of decades of inaction."

Yet the gap between scientific urgency and political response remains wide. Several major economies have delayed or diluted their emissions reduction targets, citing economic pressures and energy security concerns. Meanwhile, investment in renewable energy, while growing, still falls short of the levels needed to limit warming to manageable levels. Carbon dioxide concentrations in the atmosphere continue to rise, exceeding 425 parts per million — a concentration not seen in at least three million years.

The WMO report is careful to note that individual years above 1.5 degrees do not constitute a permanent breach of the Paris target, which is defined as a long-term average. But the distinction offers little comfort. Each fraction of a degree intensifies the impacts: more lethal heatwaves, more destructive floods, longer droughts, and faster sea-level rise. The window for limiting damage is narrowing, and the data suggests it is closing faster than most models previously anticipated.

## 长难句解析

**1.** The probability that the five-year average temperature will exceed the critical 1.5 degrees Celsius threshold above pre-industrial levels has risen to 75 per cent, a figure that would have been considered alarmist merely a decade ago.

- **主句**: The probability has risen to 75 per cent
- **同位语从句**: that the five-year average temperature will exceed the critical 1.5 degrees Celsius threshold above pre-industrial levels（解释 probability 的内容）
- **同位语**: a figure = 75 per cent
- **定语从句**: that would have been considered alarmist merely a decade ago（修饰 a figure，虚拟语气表示与过去事实相反）
- **翻译**: 五年平均气温超过工业化前水平1.5摄氏度这一关键阈值的概率已上升至75%，这一数字在仅仅十年前还会被视为危言耸听。

**2.** Scientists warn that the combination of long-term warming trends and a strong El Niño could push 2027 to unprecedented temperatures, with cascading consequences for agriculture, water supplies, and public health across the tropics and subtropics.

- **主句**: Scientists warn that...
- **宾语从句**: the combination...could push 2027 to unprecedented temperatures
- **伴随状语**: with cascading consequences for agriculture, water supplies, and public health across the tropics and subtropics
- **翻译**: 科学家警告，长期变暖趋势与强厄尔尼诺现象的结合可能将2027年的气温推至前所未有的水平，对热带和亚热带的农业、供水和公共卫生产生连锁后果。

世界气象组织发布严酷评估：2020年代末前创最热年份纪录的概率为86%，五年均温超过1.5°C的概率达75%。海洋温度反复破纪录，加速冰川融化。预计2026年末的厄尔尼诺可能使2027年达到前所未有的高温。联合国气候负责人称当前热浪是"残酷提醒"，呼吁加速淘汰化石燃料。但政治回应仍滞后于科学紧迫性，CO₂浓度已超过425ppm。每一度的升温都在加剧影响，限制损害的窗口正在加速关闭。

## 思考题

**1.** According to the WMO report, what does the 75 per cent probability refer to?

A. The chance that global temperatures will decrease by 2027.

B. The likelihood that the five-year average temperature will surpass 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels.

C. The probability that all countries will meet their Paris Agreement targets.

D. The chance that El Niño will occur in late 2026.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文明确指出"The probability that the five-year average temperature will exceed the critical 1.5 degrees Celsius threshold above pre-industrial levels has risen to 75 per cent"。A与原文相反；C原文未提及；D厄尔尼诺是"expected"而非概率为75%。
</details>

**2.** Why does the UN climate chief describe the current heatwaves as a "brutal reminder"?

A. To suggest that heatwaves are unusual and temporary events.

B. To emphasise that the extreme heat is a predictable result of decades of inaction on climate change.

C. To argue that technology alone can solve the climate crisis.

D. To downplay the seriousness of rising temperatures.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文指出"We are not witnessing an aberration...We are witnessing the predictable outcome of decades of inaction"，即高温不是反常现象，而是数十年不作为的可预见结果。A与"not an aberration"矛盾；C原文未提及技术解决；D与"brutal reminder"的严肃语气相反。
</details>

**3.** The passage suggests that the distinction between a single year above 1.5°C and a long-term average above 1.5°C ______.

A. means the Paris target has already been permanently breached

B. provides little comfort because each fraction of a degree intensifies impacts

C. proves that climate models have been overly pessimistic

D. reduces the urgency of cutting emissions

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文说"But the distinction offers little comfort. Each fraction of a degree intensifies the impacts"，即这种区分并不能带来多少安慰，因为每一度的升温都在加剧影响。A与原文"do not constitute a permanent breach"矛盾；C原文未提及模型悲观；D与文章紧迫基调相反。
</details>
