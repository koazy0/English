# 2019 Text 4 — 法国数字税与科技巨头

## 正文

In July 2019, France passed a landmark law imposing a three percent tax on the revenue of large technology companies — a measure that ignited a transatlantic trade dispute and exposed a fundamental flaw in the international tax system. The French digital tax was not merely about money; it was about fairness in an economy that has been transformed by technology.

The problem is well documented. Companies like Google, Amazon, Facebook, and Apple generate enormous revenues in countries where they have minimal physical presence. By channelling profits through low-tax jurisdictions such as Ireland, Luxembourg, and the Netherlands, they pay effective tax rates far below those borne by domestic businesses. In the European Union, digital firms pay an average effective tax rate of roughly 9.5 percent, compared with 23.2 percent for traditional companies. This disparity angers both taxpayers and governments, who see multinational tech giants benefiting from public infrastructure and educated workforces while contributing little to their upkeep.

France decided to act unilaterally after years of failed international negotiations. The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) had been trying to broker a global agreement on digital taxation since 2015, but progress was glacial. The United States, home to most of the affected companies, resisted any framework that would increase its firms' tax burden. France argued that waiting for consensus was an excuse for inaction and that its national tax was a necessary stopgap until a global deal could be reached.

The American response was swift and fierce. The Trump administration launched an investigation under Section 301 of the Trade Act, threatening retaliatory tariffs on French goods worth roughly 2.4 billion dollars, including wine, cheese, and luxury handbags. The US trade representative called the French tax "unreasonable, discriminatory, and burdensome." France stood firm, with President Macron insisting that sovereignty over taxation was non-negotiable. The standoff risked escalating into a broader trade war at a time when the global economy was already fragile.

Other countries watched carefully — and many followed France's lead. The United Kingdom, Italy, Spain, and India all announced or implemented their own digital taxes. This proliferation of national measures, while politically popular, created a patchwork of overlapping regulations that complicated compliance for businesses and increased the risk of double taxation. The OECD warned that without a coordinated global solution, the international tax system could fragment, harming trade and investment worldwide.

Ultimately, the digital tax debate reflects a deeper question: how should the global economy tax economic activity that knows no borders? The current system, designed in the 1920s for a world of physical goods and fixed establishments, is poorly suited to the digital age. Reform is inevitable. The only question is whether it will come through cooperation or conflict. France's gamble was that bold action would force the world to confront this reality. Whether that bet succeeds remains to be seen.

## 长难句解析

**1.** By channelling profits through low-tax jurisdictions such as Ireland, Luxembourg, and the Netherlands, they pay effective tax rates far below those borne by domestic businesses.

- **拆分**：By channelling... 为方式状语，主句为 they pay effective tax rates，far below those 为比较结构，those 指代 tax rates，borne by 为过去分词作后置定语。
- **翻译**：通过将利润转入爱尔兰、卢森堡和荷兰等低税管辖区，他们支付的实际税率远低于本国企业所承担的税率。

**2.** The current system, designed in the 1920s for a world of physical goods and fixed establishments, is poorly suited to the digital age.

- **拆分**：主语 the current system，谓语 is poorly suited to，过去分词短语 designed in... 作非限制性定语，说明该系统的时代背景。
- **翻译**：当前体系是1920年代为实体商品和固定场所的世界而设计的，极不适应数字时代。

## 文章大意

本文聚焦法国2019年开征数字税引发的国际争端。科技巨头在各国赚取巨额收入却通过低税管辖区避税，有效税率仅为9.5%，远低于传统企业的23.2%。法国在OECD多年谈判无果后单方面征税，美国则以报复性关税威胁回应，引发贸易对峙。英国、意大利等国随后效仿法国，但各国自征数字税导致监管碎片化风险。文章指出，这一争论的实质是：如何对没有国界的数字经济活动征税？现行国际税收体系已不适配数字时代，改革不可避免，关键在于通过合作还是冲突来实现。
