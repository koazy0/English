# Huawei's Chip Queen and the New Law Replacing Moore's

## 正文

When Huawei's semiconductor division HiSilicon announced what it calls "Tau's Scaling Law," the declaration carried more than technical significance. It was a statement of defiance. Since United States sanctions blocked Huawei's access to Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company's advanced foundries in 2019, the Chinese technology giant has been forced to chart an entirely different path to chip advancement. Rather than pursuing the shrinking transistor densities that define Moore's Law, Huawei is betting on system-level optimization — and claiming it can reach equivalent performance through architectural innovation alone.

The premise of Tau's Scaling Law is that the performance of a computing system can double approximately every two years not by miniaturising individual transistors but by reimagining how those transistors are organised, interconnected, and coordinated. This encompasses advanced packaging techniques that stack chiplets vertically, novel interconnect fabrics that reduce data movement latency, and software-hardware co-design strategies that tailor silicon to specific workloads with unprecedented precision. According to Huawei, this approach will enable its next-generation processors to achieve performance equivalent to a 1.4-nanometre process node by 2031, despite being manufactured on older lithography equipment.

Industry analysts are divided on the credibility of this claim. Some note that system-level optimization has indeed delivered significant gains elsewhere — Apple's M-series processors achieved remarkable performance-per-watt improvements partly through sophisticated packaging and memory architecture, rather than raw transistor shrinkage alone. If Huawei can replicate and extend such techniques, the argument goes, narrowing the gap with TSMC from more than five years to approximately three is plausible.

Sceptics counter that packaging and architecture cannot indefinitely compensate for lithographic limitations. Transistor density remains the foundational constraint: no amount of clever interconnection can overcome the physical reality that fewer transistors mean fewer parallel operations and lower peak throughput. Moreover, Huawei's access to cutting-edge EUV lithography equipment remains restricted, and domestic alternatives from Shanghai Micro Electronics Equipment are widely regarded as at least a generation behind ASML's latest systems. The 1.4-nanometre equivalence claim, critics argue, relies on selective benchmarks that favour Huawei's specific design choices.

The geopolitical implications are substantial. If Tau's Scaling Law proves viable, it would demonstrate that export controls cannot permanently constrain technological advancement — only redirect it. A China capable of producing competitive chips without Western manufacturing equipment would fundamentally alter the strategic calculus of the US-China technology rivalry. Conversely, if the claim proves inflated, it would reinforce the argument that semiconductor sanctions remain an effective lever of Western policy.

What is certain is that the era of a single, universally accepted scaling law is ending. The future of computing performance will be defined not by one path but by competing philosophies, each shaped by the constraints and ambitions of the nations that pursue them.

## 文章大意（中文概括）

华为海思宣布"Tau标度定律"替代摩尔定律，因美国制裁无法使用台积电先进制程，转而走系统级优化路线：通过先进封装、垂直堆叠小芯片、新型互连结构和软硬协同设计来提升性能。华为声称2031年前可达1.4nm等效性能，将与台积电差距从5年缩至3年。支持者以苹果M系列芯片为例证；质疑者认为封装不能无限弥补光刻局限，且1.4nm等效说法依赖选择性基准。地缘政治影响深远：成功则证明出口管制只能改变技术路径而非阻止进步；失败则证明制裁仍是有效手段。

## 思考题

**1.** According to the passage, what is the key difference between Tau's Scaling Law and Moore's Law?

A. Tau's Law focuses on reducing transistor size, while Moore's Law focuses on system optimization.

B. Tau's Law achieves performance gains through system-level organization rather than transistor miniaturization.

C. Tau's Law applies only to mobile processors, while Moore's Law applies to all chips.

D. Tau's Law predicts faster performance gains than Moore's Law originally did.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。文章第二段明确指出Tau's Scaling Law的核心是"not by miniaturising individual transistors but by reimagining how those transistors are organised, interconnected, and coordinated"，即通过系统级组织而非晶体管微型化来提升性能。A将两者关系颠倒；C原文未提及移动处理器限制；D原文未比较两者速度。
</details>
