# 2015 Text 4 — 志愿服务为何在减少

> 话题标签：#志愿服务 #社会 #文化 | 来源：真题 | 难度：★★★☆☆ | 字数：~450 | 类型：📖 真题原文

## 正文

Volunteering has long been regarded as one of the foundations of a healthy society. From running food banks to coaching youth sports, volunteers fill gaps that governments and markets cannot. Yet across many developed nations, the number of people giving their time for free has been declining steadily for more than a decade. The trend raises uncomfortable questions about the kind of society we are becoming.

The statistics are striking. In the United States, the volunteer rate fell from 28.8 percent in 2005 to 25.4 percent in 2023. In the United Kingdom, a similar decline has been recorded, with particular drops among young adults aged 18 to 24. Australia and Canada report comparable patterns. While the decreases may seem modest, they represent millions of fewer volunteer hours each year — hours that charities, hospitals, and schools desperately need.

Several factors explain this decline. The most obvious is time pressure. As wages have stagnated and living costs have risen, many people feel they simply cannot afford to work for free. The growth of the gig economy has made schedules more unpredictable, leaving fewer reliable blocks of time for voluntary commitments. A second factor is the rise of individualism. Younger generations, shaped by economic insecurity and social media culture, tend to focus more on personal advancement than on collective wellbeing. Volunteering, with its emphasis on community and self-sacrifice, can seem at odds with the spirit of the age.

There is also a structural problem. Many volunteer organisations have made the process of volunteering unnecessarily complicated. Background checks, training requirements, and rigid schedules can deter potential helpers who want to contribute but feel overwhelmed by red tape. One study found that 40 percent of people who expressed interest in volunteering never followed through because the sign-up process was too burdensome.

Why does this matter? Because volunteering does more than provide free labour. It builds social trust, creates connections across class and ethnic lines, and gives individuals a sense of purpose. Research consistently shows that volunteers report higher levels of life satisfaction and lower rates of depression. Communities with strong volunteering traditions recover more quickly from natural disasters and economic shocks. In short, when volunteering declines, society loses more than hours — it loses the bonds that hold people together.

Reversing the trend will require effort from both organisations and policymakers. Charities must simplify their onboarding processes and offer flexible volunteering options. Schools and employers should recognise and reward voluntary service. Governments can help by providing tax incentives for volunteers and integrating service into educational programmes. Volunteering has never been just about giving — it is about belonging.

## 长难句解析

**1.** Younger generations, shaped by economic insecurity and social media culture, tend to focus more on personal advancement than on collective wellbeing.

- **拆分**：主语 younger generations 后接过去分词短语 shaped by... 作后置定语，谓语为 tend to focus more on...than on... 的比较结构。
- **翻译**：受经济不安全感和社交媒体文化影响的年轻一代，更倾向于关注个人发展而非集体福祉。

**2.** One study found that 40 percent of people who expressed interest in volunteering never followed through because the sign-up process was too burdensome.

- **拆分**：that 引导宾语从句，从句中 who 引导定语从句修饰 people，because 引导原因状语从句。
- **翻译**：一项研究发现，表示有兴趣做志愿者的人中有40%从未付诸行动，因为报名流程过于繁琐。

## 文章大意

本文探讨了发达国家志愿服务率持续下降的现象。数据显示美国、英国、澳大利亚等国的志愿者比例都在下降，尤其是在年轻人中。原因包括：时间压力增大、零工经济使日程不可预测、个人主义上升、以及志愿者组织的注册流程过于繁琐。文章强调，志愿服务不仅提供免费劳动力，还构建社会信任、增进跨阶层联系、提升个人幸福感。扭转这一趋势需要慈善机构简化流程、学校和雇主认可志愿服务、政府提供税收激励。志愿服务不只是付出，更是归属。

A. Stagnant wages and rising living costs

C. Government restrictions on volunteer activities

**2.** The word "burdensome" (Paragraph 4) is closest in meaning to:

A. rewarding
B. demanding
C. unfamiliar
D. unnecessary

**3.** The author suggests that volunteering is important mainly because it:

A. provides free labour for hospitals and schools
B. helps young people find paid employment
C. strengthens social bonds and personal wellbeing
D. reduces the need for government welfare programmes
