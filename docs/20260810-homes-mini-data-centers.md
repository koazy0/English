## 正文

The race to build artificial intelligence infrastructure has encountered an unexpected bottleneck, and it is not a shortage of chips. It is the electricity grid. Large data centres, each consuming as much power as a small city, are increasingly difficult to connect to an aging electrical infrastructure that was never designed for such demand. A partnership between Span, a home energy management company, and Nvidia, the dominant supplier of AI processors, proposes a radical workaround: turn homes into mini data centres.

Their joint project, called XFRA, installs computing nodes in residential buildings. Each node contains 16 high-performance GPUs and draws approximately 12.5 kilowatts of power — roughly equivalent to running a large air conditioning unit continuously. The concept exploits a simple observation: most homes have significant unused electrical capacity. A typical household circuit panel is rated for 200 amps, but average usage rarely exceeds 30 per cent of that capacity at any given time. XFRA captures the surplus to run AI workloads.

The advantages extend beyond merely bypassing grid constraints. Distributed computing reduces the need for massive new data centre construction, which faces lengthy permitting processes and community opposition in many regions. It also brings computation closer to users, potentially reducing latency for certain applications. And because the nodes are dispersed across thousands of locations, the system is inherently more resilient than a centralized facility — a single hardware failure or power outage affects only a tiny fraction of total capacity.

Unsurprisingly, the concept raises concerns. Homeowners may be uneasy about hosting equipment that consumes 12.5 kilowatts and generates substantial heat, even if they are compensated for the electricity and offered discounted AI services in return. There are questions about noise from cooling fans, the risk of equipment malfunction in uncontrolled environments, and the implications for home insurance policies. Security is another worry: a distributed network of computing nodes presents a far larger attack surface than a fortified data centre with controlled physical access.

Energy analysts also point out a timing mismatch. Residential electricity demand peaks in the early evening when people return home and turn on appliances, precisely when computing demand for AI inference also surges. Rather than smoothing demand, distributed nodes could intensify peak load, straining local transformers and distribution lines that were not designed for the additional burden.

Despite these challenges, the underlying logic is compelling. The demand for AI computing capacity is growing far faster than the grid's ability to supply it, and the gap is widening. Traditional data centres take years to plan, permit, and build. XFRA nodes can be installed in weeks. If the technical and social hurdles can be overcome, the living room may become the unlikely frontier of the AI revolution.

## 长难句解析

**1.** A partnership between Span, a home energy management company, and Nvidia, the dominant supplier of AI processors, proposes a radical workaround: turn homes into mini data centres.

- **主句**: A partnership proposes a radical workaround
- **同位语 1**: Span, a home energy management company
- **同位语 2**: Nvidia, the dominant supplier of AI processors
- **冒号解释**: turn homes into mini data centres
- **翻译**: 家庭能源管理公司Span与AI处理器主导供应商Nvidia的合作伙伴关系提出了一种激进的变通方案：把家庭变成微型数据中心。

**2.** Rather than smoothing demand, distributed nodes could intensify peak load, straining local transformers and distribution lines that were not designed for the additional burden.

- **状语**: Rather than smoothing demand（而非平抑需求）
- **主句**: distributed nodes could intensify peak load
- **结果状语**: straining local transformers and distribution lines（现在分词表结果）
- **定语从句**: that were not designed for the additional burden（修饰 transformers and distribution lines）
- **翻译**: 分布式节点非但不能平抑需求，反而可能加剧峰值负荷，使原本未针对额外负担设计的当地变压器和配电线路承受压力。

## 文章大意（中文概括）

Span与Nvidia合作推出XFRA项目，将AI计算节点安装在家中。每节点16块GPU、耗电12.5kW，利用家庭闲置电力容量运行AI任务，绕过电网瓶颈。优势包括避免大型数据中心建设、降低延迟、提高韧性。但担忧包括：设备发热与噪音、故障风险、保险问题、安全攻击面增大，以及家庭用电与AI推理需求同时达峰的错配问题。然而AI算力需求增长远超电网供电能力，传统数据中心建设需数年，XFRA节点几周即可安装——客厅可能成为AI革命的前沿。

A. A shortage of GPU chips.

B. Insufficient electrical grid capacity.

C. Lack of qualified data centre engineers.

D. Government regulations on AI development.

<summary>答案与解析</summary>

</details>

**2.** Why does the XFRA concept raise security concerns?

A. Because the computing nodes use outdated encryption.

B. Because a distributed network presents a larger attack surface than a centralized facility.

C. Because homeowners cannot be trusted to keep the equipment safe.

D. Because the nodes are connected to military networks.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文指出"a distributed network of computing nodes presents a far larger attack surface than a fortified data centre with controlled physical access"，即分布式网络的攻击面更大。A原文未提及加密问题；C表述不当；D原文未提及军事网络。
</details>

**3.** What timing problem do energy analysts identify with the XFRA model?

A. AI computing demand and residential electricity demand peak at different times.

D. Homeowners refuse to run the nodes during weekends.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文指出"Residential electricity demand peaks in the early evening...precisely when computing demand for AI inference also surges"，两者同时达峰会加剧负荷。A说不同时，与原文矛盾；C原文未提及夜间限制；D原文未提及周末。
