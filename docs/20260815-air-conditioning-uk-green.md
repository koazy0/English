# Can Air Conditioning Go Green Before It Heats the Planet?

## 正文

Air conditioning was once a luxury in Britain. No longer. Ownership has doubled in just three years, with an estimated four million homes now equipped with cooling units. As summer temperatures regularly breach 30 degrees Celsius across southern England, the rush to install AC has become a familiar seasonal ritual. But the convenience comes with an environmental cost that is becoming harder to ignore.

The figures are sobering. A standard domestic air conditioning unit running for eight hours a day produces approximately 4.87 kilograms of carbon dioxide, roughly equivalent to driving a car 25 kilometres. Multiplied across four million homes, the collective emissions are substantial — and growing. The International Energy Agency estimates that air conditioning already accounts for nearly 20 per cent of electricity consumption in buildings worldwide, and that share is rising fast as incomes increase in hotter countries.

The problem is compounded by what engineers call a positive feedback loop: warmer temperatures drive more air conditioning use, which increases energy demand, which raises emissions, which contributes to further warming. Under current trends, the IEA projects that global energy demand for space cooling will triple by 2050. In Britain specifically, climate models suggest that 22 per cent of homes would require active cooling if global temperatures rise by 2 degrees Celsius above pre-industrial levels.

Yet there are reasons for cautious optimism. The most promising approach pairs air conditioning with solar power. Since peak cooling demand coincides with peak solar generation — both occur during the hottest, sunniest hours — rooftop solar panels can offset much of the electricity that AC units consume. Studies in southern Europe have shown that well-designed solar-plus-AC systems can reduce grid electricity consumption for cooling by up to 70 per cent during summer months.

Strategic timing offers another lever. Smart thermostats and time-of-use electricity pricing can shift some cooling demand to periods when renewable energy is abundant and grid carbon intensity is low. Pre-cooling a home during the afternoon, when solar generation peaks, can reduce the need for intensive cooling during the evening, when demand surges but solar output declines.

Building design also plays a critical role. Passive cooling techniques — improved insulation, reflective roofing materials, natural ventilation corridors, and green spaces that reduce the urban heat island effect — can significantly reduce the need for mechanical cooling. A well-insulated home with strategic shading may require half the air conditioning of an identical building without these features.

The question is not whether Britain will adopt more air conditioning — it already has. The question is whether that adoption can be managed wisely enough to avoid turning a response to climate change into an accelerator of it. The technology to break the feedback loop exists. What remains uncertain is whether policy and investment will keep pace with the thermostats.

## 长难句解析

**1.** The problem is compounded by what engineers call a positive feedback loop: warmer temperatures drive more air conditioning use, which increases energy demand, which raises emissions, which contributes to further warming.

- **主句**: The problem is compounded by what engineers call a positive feedback loop
- **宾语从句**: what engineers call a positive feedback loop
- **冒号解释**: warmer temperatures → more AC use → increased energy demand → raised emissions → further warming（链式因果）
- **三级定语从句**: which increases... which raises... which contributes to...（依次修饰前一个分句的结果）
- **翻译**: 工程师所说的正反馈循环加剧了这一问题：气温升高推动更多空调使用，增加能源需求，推高排放，进一步加剧变暖。

**2.** Since peak cooling demand coincides with peak solar generation — both occur during the hottest, sunniest hours — rooftop solar panels can offset much of the electricity that AC units consume.

- **原因状语从句**: Since peak cooling demand coincides with peak solar generation
- **破折号解释**: both occur during the hottest, sunniest hours
- **主句**: rooftop solar panels can offset much of the electricity
- **定语从句**: that AC units consume（修饰 electricity）
- **翻译**: 由于峰值制冷需求与峰值太阳能发电同时出现——两者都发生在最热、阳光最充足的时间——屋顶太阳能板可以抵消空调消耗的大部分电力。

## 文章大意（中文概括）

英国空调拥有量三年翻番至400万户，但空调运行8小时排放4.87kg CO₂，全球建筑用电20%来自空调。更严重的是正反馈循环：越热越开空调→排放增加→更热。IEA预测2050年全球制冷能源需求将增至三倍。但解决方案存在：太阳能与空调配对（需求与发电高峰重合，可减排70%）、智能恒温器和分时电价调节需求、被动冷却技术（隔热、反射屋顶、自然通风、绿地）可减少50%制冷需求。问题不是英国是否会用更多空调，而是能否避免让应对气候变化的工具变成加速气候变化的因素。

## 思考题

**1.** What "positive feedback loop" does the passage describe in relation to air conditioning?

A. Better AC technology leads to lower prices, which increases sales.

B. Warmer temperatures increase AC use, which raises emissions and causes further warming.

C. More AC units create jobs, which increases economic growth.

D. Solar panels become cheaper as more people install them.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文明确描述正反馈循环为"warmer temperatures drive more air conditioning use, which increases energy demand, which raises emissions, which contributes to further warming"。A是价格循环，非原文描述；C是经济循环，非环境反馈；D与空调无关。
</details>

**2.** Why is pairing air conditioning with solar power considered particularly effective?

A. Because solar panels work best at night when cooling demand is highest.

B. Because peak cooling demand and peak solar generation occur at the same time.

C. Because solar energy can store excess power for winter use.

D. Because solar panels cool the building directly through shading.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>B</b>。原文指出"peak cooling demand coincides with peak solar generation — both occur during the hottest, sunniest hours"，因此太阳能可以抵消空调的大部分用电。A错误（太阳能夜间不工作）；C原文未提及冬季储能；D原文未提及遮阳效果。
</details>

**3.** According to the passage, what is the central uncertainty about air conditioning adoption in Britain?

A. Whether the technology will become affordable for most households.

B. Whether people will accept passive cooling as an alternative.

C. Whether policy and investment can keep pace with the growing demand for cooling.

D. Whether air conditioning will be banned by the government.

<details>
<summary>答案与解析</summary>
<b>C</b>。文章末句点明"What remains uncertain is whether policy and investment will keep pace with the thermostats"，即政策和投资能否跟上空调需求的增长。A原文未提及负担能力；B原文未讨论接受度问题；D原文未提及禁令。
</details>
