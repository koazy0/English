# Young People Are Falling Behind, but Not Because of AI

>

## 正文

The job market for young people is brutal. Since ChatGPT was released in late 2022, the unemployment rate for recent college graduates has shot up to nearly six percent. Headlines warn that artificial intelligence is stealing entry-level jobs, and the narrative feels convincing: machines that can write, code, and analyze data must surely be displacing the newcomers who once performed those tasks. But that story is wrong — or at least premature.

Economists Adam Ozimek and Nathan Goldschlag decided to test the popular claim. They noticed something odd in the data: many young workers without college degrees had simply stopped looking for work altogether. When people give up searching, they are no longer counted as unemployed, which artificially lowers the jobless rate for their group. That, in turn, makes college graduates look worse by comparison, creating the illusion that degree-holders are the primary victims. Once the researchers applied a broader measure — one that includes those who have dropped out of the labor force entirely — the picture looked very different. Non-college young people were faring even worse than their degreed peers. As Ozimek put it, "It turns out the labor market for young people — all young people — is even worse than we thought."

If AI is not the main culprit, then what is? The answer lies in a broader economic slowdown. Hiring across the economy has slowed to levels not seen since the aftermath of the Great Recession. Employers, uncertain about the direction of the economy, are reluctant to add workers. Rising tariffs, stubborn inflation, and shifting trade policies have only deepened their caution. Existing employees, meanwhile, are staying put in their current roles, further reducing the number of openings available to newcomers. In such an environment, young job-seekers suffer disproportionately — not because technology has rendered them obsolete, but because hiring young people is fundamentally an investment in the future. As Goldschlag explained, "Hiring young people doesn't bring benefits right away — it's really an investment in the future. So if you're unsure about what that future will look like, that's one of the first things you stop doing."

This distinction matters a great deal. Blaming AI for youth unemployment may be emotionally satisfying, but it directs attention away from the real problem: an economy gripped by uncertainty, in which employers would rather wait than invest in the next generation. Policy solutions that address economic confidence — stabilizing trade policy, controlling inflation, and restoring business optimism — would do far more for young workers than any attempt to slow down artificial intelligence. Until confidence returns, young people will continue to fall behind — and no amount of AI regulation will fix that.

## 长难句解析

### 1

> When people give up searching, they are no longer counted as unemployed, which artificially lowers the jobless rate for their group, creating the illusion that degree-holders are the primary victims.

**结构拆解**：

- **时间状语从句**：When people give up searching（当人们放弃找工作时）
- **主干**：they are no longer counted as unemployed（他们不再被计入失业人口）
- **非限制性定语从句**：which artificially lowers the jobless rate for their group（which 指代前句内容，"这人为地降低了该群体的失业率"）
- **现在分词短语**：creating the illusion that degree-holders are the primary victims（表结果，"从而制造了一种错觉"）
  - that 引导同位语从句，解释 illusion 的内容

**翻译**：当人们放弃找工作时，他们不再被计入失业人口，这人为地降低了该群体的失业率，从而制造出一种大学毕业生是主要受害者的错觉。

### 2

> Not because technology has rendered them obsolete, but because hiring young people is fundamentally an investment in the future.

**结构拆解**：

- **not because... but because...**：不是因为……而是因为……（并列原因状语从句）
- **前从句**：technology has rendered them obsolete（render + 宾语 + 宾补，"使它们过时"）
- **后从句**：hiring young people is fundamentally an investment in the future（动名词短语 hiring young people 作主语）

**翻译**：不是因为技术使他们变得过时，而是因为雇用年轻人本质上是对未来的一项投资。

## 文章大意

本文反驳了"AI导致青年失业"的流行观点。经济学家Ozimek和Goldschlag发现，许多无大学学历的年轻人已退出劳动力市场，不再被计入失业统计，这人为地让大学毕业生看起来处境更差。采用更宽泛的衡量标准后，所有年轻人群体的就业状况都比预想的更糟。文章指出，真正的原因是经济不确定性导致招聘放缓至大衰退后的水平——雇用年轻人是对未来的投资，而雇主对未来缺乏信心时，这便是他们最先停止做的事。
