# Diffusion Models Beat GANs on Image Synthesis

> **论文**: Diffusion Models Beat GANs on Image Synthesis
> **作者**: Dhariwal & Nichol (OpenAI)
> **会议**: CVPR 2021
> **日期**: 2026-07-23

---

## 正文

Generative adversarial networks, or GANs, have long dominated image synthesis, producing photorealistic samples that seemed to set the standard for generative modelling. Yet GANs suffer from well-documented limitations: training instability, mode collapse, and difficulty in capturing the full diversity of the training distribution. Diffusion models, which generate images through an iterative denoising process, have emerged as a compelling alternative—one that, as we demonstrate, can surpass GANs on key quality metrics.

A diffusion model works by gradually adding Gaussian noise to an image over many steps until it becomes pure noise, then learning to reverse this process. Starting from random noise, the model denoises step by step, gradually recovering structure and detail until a coherent image emerges. Each step is small and manageable, making the learning problem tractable. The reverse process is parameterised as a neural network that predicts the noise added at each step, and training proceeds by optimising a simple mean-squared error objective.

The quality of samples generated by diffusion models depends critically on how the model is guided during sampling. Classifier guidance is our key innovation. By training a separate classifier on noisy images and using its gradients to steer the denoising process, we can dramatically improve sample quality. The classifier effectively tells the model, at each denoising step, how to adjust its output to be more consistent with a desired class or feature. This guidance can be controlled by a scale parameter: higher guidance strength produces images that are more class-consistent but less diverse, while lower strength preserves diversity at the cost of some fidelity.

Our experiments on ImageNet demonstrate the effectiveness of this approach. With classifier guidance, diffusion models achieve a Fréchet Inception Distance of 4.59 on class-conditional ImageNet generation, outperforming the best GAN models even when those models are allowed to cherry-pick their best samples. On unconditional generation benchmarks, the advantage is even more pronounced. Human evaluators also consistently prefer samples from guided diffusion models over those from GANs, finding them more photorealistic and diverse.

The iterative nature of diffusion models offers another advantage over GANs: greater controllability. Because the generation process unfolds over hundreds of steps, it is possible to intervene at intermediate stages—to modify the image being generated, inject new features, or combine elements from different sources. This flexibility has made diffusion models the foundation for a new generation of creative tools, including text-to-image systems like DALL-E 2 and Stable Diffusion, which would have been far more difficult to build using GAN architectures.

The trade-off is speed. A GAN generates images in a single forward pass, while a diffusion model requires hundreds of denoising steps. This makes diffusion models substantially slower at inference time, a limitation that has spurred considerable research into acceleration techniques. Recent work on consistency models and distilled diffusion has reduced the required steps dramatically, narrowing the speed gap while preserving quality.

The broader lesson is that generative modelling does not require adversarial training. The competitive dynamics of GANs—the generator and discriminator locked in an escalating contest—while conceptually elegant, introduce instabilities that are not necessary for high-quality generation. Diffusion models achieve superior results with a simpler, more stable training objective, demonstrating that in generative AI, patience in the form of iterative refinement can outperform the adversarial shortcut.

## 长难句解析

**1.** "By training a separate classifier on noisy images and using its gradients to steer the denoising process, we can dramatically improve sample quality."

- **主干**: we can dramatically improve sample quality
- **By training a separate classifier on noisy images and using its gradients to steer the denoising process** 是方式状语，由两个并列的动名词短语组成：training 和 using。
- **steer the denoising process** 意为"引导去噪过程"。
- **翻译**: 通过在有噪声的图像上训练一个单独的分类器，并使用其梯度来引导去噪过程，我们可以显著提高样本质量。

**2.** "The competitive dynamics of GANs—the generator and discriminator locked in an escalating contest—while conceptually elegant, introduce instabilities that are not necessary for high-quality generation."

- **主干**: The competitive dynamics of GANs introduce instabilities
- 破折号之间 **the generator and discriminator locked in an escalating contest** 是对 competitive dynamics 的同位语解释。
- **while conceptually elegant** 是让步状语，表示"虽然在概念上很优雅"。
- **that are not necessary for high-quality generation** 是定语从句，修饰 instabilities。
- **翻译**: GAN 的竞争动态——生成器和判判器锁定在不断升级的竞争中——虽然在概念上很优雅，但引入了高质量生成所不必要的训练不稳定性。

## 文章大意

本文介绍扩散模型在图像合成任务上超越 GAN 的突破。扩散模型通过迭代去噪过程生成图像，逐步从纯噪声恢复出清晰图像。核心创新是分类器引导——利用分类器梯度在每步去噪中调整输出方向，显著提升样本质量。实验显示引导扩散模型在 FID 指标和人类评估中均优于 GAN。迭代过程的另一优势是可控性更高，催生了 DALL-E 2 等文本到图像系统。代价是推理速度较慢，但蒸馏等技术正在缩小差距。核心启示：高质量生成不必依赖对抗训练，迭代精炼可以超越对抗捷径。
