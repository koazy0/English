# Illinois Passes America's First AI Safety Audit Law

> **Date**: 20260718 · **Source**: Adapted from Wired (May 2026) · **Difficulty**: ★★★☆☆ · **Type**: 📰 同源外刊精选
>
> **Topic**: #科技 #政策 #法律

---

In a move that could reshape how artificial intelligence is governed in the United States, Illinois has enacted Senate Bill 315, becoming the first state to require independent third-party audits of frontier AI labs' safety practices. The law, signed by Governor JB Pritzker in May 2026, mandates that any company operating a large-scale AI model with more than ten billion parameters must submit to an annual audit conducted by an accredited, independent body.

The legislation emerged from growing concerns that AI companies have been effectively policing themselves. Despite the enormous societal impact of frontier models—from their role in hiring decisions and criminal justice to their capacity for generating disinformation—no federal regulation currently requires these companies to demonstrate that their safety measures actually work. Internal red-teaming exercises, in which companies test their own models for dangerous capabilities, have been criticised as insufficiently rigorous and inherently conflicted. After all, a company evaluating its own product has every incentive to downplay risks.

SB 315 addresses this conflict by requiring audits from organisations with no financial ties to the AI labs they examine. The audits must assess a range of safety dimensions, including bias and fairness, propensity to generate harmful content, robustness against adversarial manipulation, and the adequacy of measures to prevent misuse. Audit results must be submitted to the Illinois Department of Financial and Professional Regulation, which will publish summary findings while protecting proprietary information. Companies that fail to comply face civil penalties of up to one million dollars per violation.

What makes the law particularly significant is the breadth of its support. Both OpenAI and Anthropic, the two most prominent frontier AI labs, endorsed the bill during the legislative process—a rare instance of industry players welcoming regulation they could have opposed. Their support reflects a calculation that reasonable regulation is preferable to a patchwork of inconsistent state laws or, worse, a public backlash that could lead to far more restrictive measures. It also signals a growing recognition within the industry that self-regulation has not satisfied public trust.

Not everyone is satisfied, however. Smaller AI companies have warned that compliance costs could create a barrier to entry that entrenches the dominance of large incumbents. A startup founder testified during the legislative hearings that the cost of a single audit could exceed two hundred thousand dollars—an expense that would be trivial for a company like OpenAI but devastating for a young firm. Civil liberties organisations have also raised concerns about the definition of "harmful content," arguing that it could be used to suppress legitimate speech.

Despite these criticisms, SB 315 represents a tangible shift from voluntary commitments to enforceable requirements. If the law succeeds in improving safety without stifling innovation, other states are expected to follow Illinois's lead, potentially creating a de facto national standard. The question is no longer whether AI should be audited, but how to audit it fairly, effectively, and without concentrating power further in the hands of those who already hold the most.

## 长难句解析

> **Despite the enormous societal impact of frontier models**—from their role in hiring decisions and criminal justice to their capacity for generating disinformation—**no federal regulation currently requires these companies to demonstrate that their safety measures actually work**.

- **主干**: no federal regulation currently requires these companies to demonstrate that…
- **Despite the enormous societal impact of frontier models** 是让步状语。
- 破折号之间是对 impact 的举例说明。
- **that their safety measures actually work** 是宾语从句，作 demonstrate 的宾语。
- **翻译**: 尽管前沿模型具有巨大的社会影响——从它们在招聘决策和刑事司法中的作用到它们生成虚假信息的能力——目前没有联邦法规要求这些公司证明其安全措施确实有效。

> **Their support reflects a calculation that reasonable regulation is preferable to a patchwork of inconsistent state laws or, worse, a public backlash that could lead to far more restrictive measures**.

- **主干**: Their support reflects a calculation
- **that reasonable regulation is preferable to…** 是同位语从句，解释 calculation 的内容。
- **a patchwork of inconsistent state laws** 和 **a public backlash** 是 preferable to 的两个比较对象。
- **that could lead to far more restrictive measures** 是定语从句，修饰 backlash。
- **翻译**: 他们的支持反映了一种考量：合理的监管优于各州不一致法律的拼凑，或者更糟的是，可能导致更严格措施的公众反弹。

## 文章大意

本文报道伊利诺伊州通过 SB 315 法案，成为美国首个要求前沿 AI 实验室接受独立第三方安全审计的州。该法案要求参数超过百亿的大模型公司每年接受独立审计，评估偏见公平性、有害内容生成倾向、对抗性操纵防护等维度。值得注意的是，OpenAI 和 Anthropic 均支持该法案，认为合理监管优于不一致的州法律拼凑或更严重的公众反弹。但小型公司担忧合规成本构成进入壁垒，民权组织则对"有害内容"定义表示关切。该法案标志着从自愿承诺到可执行要求的实质转变。
