# Pope Leo's Encyclical Is More Alarming Than Hopeful

| 项目 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| 日期 | 2026-10-14 |
| 类型 | 外刊精选 |
| 难度 | ★★★★★ |
| 来源 | The Atlantic |

---

Read carefully, Pope Leo XIV's encyclical on artificial intelligence is a far more alarming document than its initial reception suggested. The headlines focused on its call for AI regulation and its defense of human dignity — themes that fit comfortably within the existing discourse of AI ethics. But the substance of the document is darker, more urgent, and more radical than such summaries convey. It treats the benefits of AI as perfunctory afterthoughts and its dangers as systemic and civilizational, and it does so with a specificity that makes dismissal impossible.

The encyclical organizes its warnings into four categories, each escalating in severity. The first is unemployment and economic displacement. The Pope does not merely note that AI may eliminate certain jobs; he argues that the scale and speed of AI-driven displacement is unprecedented in human history, outpacing the capacity of societies to adapt through retraining or social safety nets. Unlike previous technological revolutions that created new categories of employment to replace those they destroyed, AI threatens to eliminate cognitive labor itself — the very capacity that distinguished human workers from machines and provided the economic foundation for the middle class.

The second category is exploitation. The encyclical describes how AI systems, trained on the unpaid creative labor of billions of people, constitute a form of extraction that mirrors colonial resource appropriation. Writers, artists, musicians, and ordinary internet users have had their intellectual output harvested without consent or compensation to build systems that will compete with them for work. The Pope frames this not as a market failure but as a moral failure — a systematic refusal to recognize the dignity of those whose contributions made AI possible.

The third category, autonomous weapons, represents the sharpest escalation. The encyclical does not call for regulation of lethal autonomous weapons; it calls for their categorical prohibition, placing them in the same moral category as chemical and biological weapons. The argument is not that autonomous weapons are unreliable — a practical objection that invites practical counterarguments about improving reliability — but that they are intrinsically evil, because they remove the moral agent from the act of killing. A machine that decides who lives and who dies, the Pope argues, represents the nullification of the very concept of moral responsibility.

The fourth and most far-reaching category is transhumanism. The encyclical warns that the project of using AI to transcend human limitations — to extend life indefinitely, to enhance cognition beyond biological constraints, to replace human frailty with machine reliability — is not merely dangerous but dehumanizing. It treats human imperfection as a problem to be solved rather than a condition to be accepted, and in doing so, it dissolves the moral framework that gives human life its meaning.

What makes the encyclical alarming rather than merely cautionary is its tone. Previous papal statements on technology have balanced warning with hope, listing risks alongside promises. This document does not. Its treatment of AI's benefits occupies roughly five percent of its length and reads as a diplomatic concession rather than a genuine conviction. The remaining ninety-five percent is an unflinching catalog of dangers, presented with a moral certainty that leaves little room for the technology's defenders to find common ground. This is not a document that invites dialogue; it is a document that draws a line.

## 核心词汇

1. **perfunctory** — adj. 敷衍的
2. **afterthought** — n. 事后补充的想法
3. **civilizational** — adj. 文明层面的
4. **unprecedented** — adj. 史无前例的
5. **extraction** — n. 榨取
6. **appropriation** — n. 挪用
7. **categorical** — adj. 无条件的
8. **prohibition** — n. 禁止
9. **nullification** — n. 废除
10. **dehumanizing** — adj. 非人化的
11. **frailty** — n. 脆弱
12. **unflinching** — adj. 不畏缩的

## 长难句解析

1. Unlike previous technological revolutions that created new categories of employment to replace those they destroyed, AI threatens to eliminate cognitive labor itself — the very capacity that distinguished human workers from machines and provided the economic foundation for the middle class.
   - **解析**：介词短语 "Unlike previous technological revolutions" 引出对比，that 引导定语从句修饰 revolutions。主句 "AI threatens to eliminate cognitive labor itself"，破折号后是 cognitive labor 的同位语，内含 that 引导的定语从句（两个谓语 distinguished 和 provided 并列）。核心论点：AI威胁的不是特定工种，而是人类区别于机器的认知能力本身。

2. A machine that decides who lives and who dies, the Pope argues, represents the nullification of the very concept of moral responsibility.
   - **解析**：主语 "A machine"，that 引导定语从句（who lives and who dies 并列），"the Pope argues" 是插入语，谓语 represents，宾语 "the nullification of the very concept of moral responsibility"。核心论点：自主武器不是技术问题而是道德责任的终结。

## 文章大意

教皇利奥十四世的AI通谕比表面看来更具警示性。通谕将AI的益处作为敷衍性补充，而以95%篇幅详细列举四大危险：一是大规模失业，AI消除认知劳动本身而非特定工种；二是剥削，AI无偿使用数十亿人的创造劳动；三是自主武器，教皇要求无条件禁止，将其与化武并列；四是超人类主义，用AI超越人类局限的计划被视为非人化。通谕的语气不是邀请对话而是划线，没有留下与AI辩护者求同存异的余地。
